The Club

Anyone can rent a boat for the day. Our members belong to one.

Legacy was built for people who love the water and have quietly outgrown the dockside cattle call.

You know the usual Miami scene: a tired boat that looked better in the listing photos, a captain you met four minutes ago, and a cooler doing most of the catering. That is not this. There is no line, no wristband, no walk-up crowd, and no stranger at the rail taking photos of your afternoon.

There is a private club with a short membership roll, a familiar crew who knows how you take your coffee, and a yacht kept the way a private club should be kept: spotless, polished, and ready before you arrive. It feels less like a day trip and more like a second residence that happens to move.

If that sounds exclusive, it is. Deliberately. Membership is a mutual fit, not a transaction. We choose carefully, our members do the same, and the upper echelon was never a large group. We intend to keep it that way.

The invitation is the ticket

Nobody stumbles into Legacy, and nobody buys their way in off the dock. Members are invited, and their guests are invited. That single rule keeps every outing exactly the right size: yours.

One yacht, one standard

Starship V is the club's only yacht, kept to a member's standard, not a rental fleet average. Professionally maintained, meticulously cleaned, same vessel, same crew, same polish, every single time you step aboard.

Fit matters, both ways

We are not right for everyone, and everyone is not right for us. That honesty protects the room. Members get captain, crew, catering, and every detail handled, and a group of people who are genuinely glad to be in the same club.

The Difference

Not your run of the mill Miami boat day

If you have spent any time on the Miami waterfront, you have seen the other version. We built Legacy to be the opposite of it, in every detail that matters.

The Usual

Booked by anyone, run for everyone

Open to whoever pays first. The boat, the crew, and the standard change every time.

  • ×A worn boat that has seen a thousand afternoons and shows every one of them
  • ×A different captain and crew each time, learning your names as you cast off
  • ×Crowded docks, loud groups, and your outing squeezed between two others
  • ×Cheap and cheerful, until you are actually aboard and can see the difference

The Legacy Way

Invited in, looked after properly

Closed to the public on purpose. One yacht, one crew, and a standard that does not move.

  • ✓Starship V, a 2022 Dyna kept immaculate and professionally maintained for members only
  • ✓The same licensed captain and crew, who know their members and how they like the day to run
  • ✓A small, considered membership roll, so the boat never feels crowded and never feels rented
  • ✓Proper catering, a set table before departure, and nothing left to chance or to a cooler
A note on fit, because it matters

Requesting an invitation is not placing an order, and an invitation is not automatic. We read every request, we have a conversation, and together we work out whether Legacy is the right club for you and whether you are the right member for Legacy. If it is not a fit, no hard feelings and no sales pitch. If it is, you will understand within minutes of stepping aboard why we protect it so carefully.
