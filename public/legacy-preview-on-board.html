On Board

Fed within minutes. Fussed over all day.

The moment Starship V leaves the dock, the table is already set, plated and waiting within minutes of departure, fully self-serve, so the afternoon belongs to the water, not the galley. What is on that table is up to you: heavy hors d'oeuvres, a full spread, or anything in between. Every outing is built around our members and their guests, their requests, their tastes, and their dietary wishes.

House signatures, to name a few

  • She-crab soup shotsOne perfect bite in a cup, finished with a drop of sherry. A house signature.
  • Mini crab cakesCrisped to order, with a remoulade drizzle, never a dip cup.
  • Guard Rice basmatiThe club serves one rice, and it is Guard.
  • A full barA full bar is available aboard, poured the way you like it.
  • Dietary concernsAny dietary concerns can be easily accommodated.
