Member & Guest Registration

Register before you come aboard

Members and their invited guests register once, before their first outing. It takes two minutes, and it includes agreeing to the club's Terms & Conditions, the liability release every guest signs before stepping aboard.

Upload a clear photo of the front of your driver's license, flat and fully in frame. Not a selfie, not a passport, not any other picture: the front of the license itself.

How the ID check works: on the live site, every uploaded license goes through an automated verification check. The system confirms the upload really is a driver's license, then matches the name and date of birth on it against the name and date of birth on this form. Anything that fails the check is flagged for manual review by the club before the registration is accepted.

Emergency Contact

Every field above is required except Comments, and registration cannot be submitted until the Liability Release is fully initialed and signed and the Terms & Conditions box is checked. On the live site, each registration is stored in the club's records and kept on file, and the club can export registrations for its contact list.

Your registration is ready

This preview prepares your registration on this device only: nothing is sent or stored, and your ID photo never leaves this page. Copy the summary below for your own records if you like. On the live site, this form submits securely to the club's own records, where the automated ID check runs and the registration is kept on file. Submitting there also sends a confirmation text message to the phone number you registered, thanking you for filling it out.
