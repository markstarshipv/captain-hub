About Legacy Yacht

Legacy Yacht delivers an elevated experience on the water in South Florida and the surrounding areas, from Miami and Biscayne Bay to Fort Lauderdale and the Palm Beaches. Every outing is planned around the guests: unhurried, polished, and personal.

Experiences

On the Water

  • Private cruises and sunset outings
  • Celebrations and special occasions
  • Corporate and club events
  • Dinner cruises to waterfront restaurants
  • Anchor, swim, and relax days on the bay
The Standard

How We Run a Yacht

A Legacy Yacht outing pairs a meticulously kept vessel with a professional captain and crew, and hospitality planned down to the details that guests actually remember: a warm welcome, a beautiful table, and a captain who treats safety and seamanship as seriously as celebration.
