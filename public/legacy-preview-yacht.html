The Yacht

Meet Starship V

Our clubhouse has a hull number. Starship V is a 63-foot Dyna flybridge yacht, berthed at Venetian Marina in Miami and kept exclusively for Legacy members and their invited guests. Never rented by the hour, never handed to strangers, and never allowed to look like it has been. Starship V was purchased brand new for our members, not passed around. One of only three Dyna 63s in the United States.
